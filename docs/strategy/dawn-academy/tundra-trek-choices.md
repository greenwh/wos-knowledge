# Tundra Trek Choices

::: info Quick take
Most Tundra Trek encounters give you two choices with different rewards. The rewards are **Books of Knowledge**, the item that upgrades Expert skills. In 20 of the 23 encounters where every choice has been tested, one choice pays **50** Books and the other pays **30**. A few pay much more (**100** or **200**), and some add or pay **Expert Sigils**. Use this page to pick the higher reward instead of guessing.
:::

## How to Use This Page

1. When an encounter opens, look at the choice buttons.
2. Search this page for the button text. On a phone, use your browser's "Find in page".
3. Pick the choice in **bold**. It has the highest reward recorded so far. If the other choice is *not yet tested*, the listed reward is the only one known.

The choice names here are short versions of the in-game button text. Search for one or two key words, not the full sentence.

## Reading the Rewards

| Entry | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **50**, 30, 100 | Books of Knowledge |
| +1 | One Expert Sigil, paid on top of the Books |
| 3 Sigils | The choice pays Expert Sigils instead of Books |
| *not yet tested* | Nobody has recorded this choice yet |

Some encounters pay more than the table shows, such as extra Sigils or other items. The table records the main reward only.

Sigils are the scarce resource for Advancing Experts, so a Sigil choice is usually worth taking. See [Resources](/strategy/dawn-academy/resources) for what Books of Knowledge and Sigils do.

## What the Table Shows

- **The 50 / 30 split is the normal case.** If an encounter is not listed, expect one choice to pay 50 Books of Knowledge and the other 30.
- **Big payouts are rare.** Only five choices recorded so far pay 100 or more: Disarm (200), Challenge to duel (100), Pass on sorting junk (100), Leave (100), and Pay full price (100).
- **"Pass" is not always the weak choice.** In one encounter, passing pays 100. In another, it pays fewer Sigils than helping.
- **Twenty choices are still untested.** The best known pick for those encounters may change once the other choice is recorded.

## Help Fill the Gaps

If you see an encounter that is not listed, or you try a choice marked *not yet tested*, note the button text and the reward shown. Send it to your alliance leadership so the table can be updated.
